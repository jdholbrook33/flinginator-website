Field manualWhat it does
(besides fling)

Your feeder already has a motor. The Flinginator 3000 gives you control of it from the cab.

01

Hold. Fling. Let go.

Hold the remote button and the spinner runs. Let go and it stops. You don't have to remember to switch anything off.

Auto-stop after 30 seconds of run time
02

Dial in the spread

While you're holding the remote, tap up to throw wider or down to drop it closer. It remembers your last setting for the next run.

Saved even with the power off
03

Full send

Double-tap and hold to run the spinner wide open. Use it for the big food plot.

Drops back to your setting on release
04

Runs on your 20V pack

Snap in the DeWalt or Milwaukee 20V battery you already own. There's no special battery to buy and no charger to keep track of.

Pick your brand's dock at checkout
05

Leave it in all season

Off really means off, so the pack can stay in from opening day to the last hunt. If you forget it on, it shuts itself off.

Auto power-off after 14 hours idle
06

Lights before daylight

Filling the hopper in the dark? Press any button and the LED strips light up the hopper for 45 seconds.

Outdoor-rated, fully molded LEDs

Treat it like a running mower. At full speed, the Flinginator 3000 throws corn hard enough to hurt. It doesn't care what's in the way, so make sure nothing is.
